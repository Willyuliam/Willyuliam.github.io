Policy-Guided Mango Shape Annealing

Apr 2026 - Now | Advisor: John H. Reif (Professor) | Code: GitHub
  • Policy-guided search: Reframed Mango's stochastic shape-annealing search as masked, constraint-aware proposal guidance for wireframe DNA origami design.
  • Action interface: Engineered a hierarchical grammar-action interface over rule, site, and parameter choices, with legal action masking and failure diagnostics to keep proposals inside feasible design space.
  • Benchmark pipeline: Built reproducible B1/B2/B3 benchmark pipelines with unified logging, constraint-failure analysis, candidate audits, and baseline comparisons.
  • Result analysis: Demonstrated PG-Mango gains over Mango ShapeAnneal and Random-Feasible on 5/5 B1-family benchmarks while preserving manufacturability constraints.
  • Release: Open-sourced the project on GitHub for reproducible inspection and future comparison.
