Invoice Reimbursement Review System

Jul 2026 | Advisor: Siwei Chen

Research and Practical Teaching Center, Chinese Aeronautical Establishment

During this internship, I developed a local reimbursement submission and review system to support student project administration, covering document processing, validation, teacher review, and report generation.

  • Student submission and teacher review: Developed a browser-based student portal that packages reimbursement information, procurement applications, invoices, verification records, and payment evidence into local ZIP submissions, together with a Python application for teacher import, review, correction, and export.
  • Document extraction: Combined PDF/Word parsing and local OCR to extract invoice numbers, dates, and amounts. Added optional vision-language model fallback for missing key fields, enabled only with explicit external-model configuration.
  • Invoice-driven validation: Used each invoice as the source for one final summary row, cross-checked invoice and verification fields, and reconciled reimbursement totals. Distinguished blocking discrepancies from warnings and provided manual confirmation after review of the originals.
  • Reporting and workflow support: Generated Excel reimbursement tables, funding-category summaries, merged supporting-document PDFs, and project archives. Preserved material associations and review records throughout the submission-to-export workflow.
  • Portable delivery: Packaged the student and teacher tools for Windows without requiring Python or Microsoft Office on the target computer, and verified submission import and Excel/PDF export after relocation.
  • Assisted Coze-based AI programming labs for Khalifa University exchange students.
